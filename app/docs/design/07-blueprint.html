Designer's note · Blueprint

Audience: professional operators and the engineers who sign off a build, plus the technician who racks the kit. The drawing set is the frame; every drawing word and every IT word is glossed on first use, so nobody has to already read drawings or know what an iLO is.

Metaphor. A drawing set, numbered in the order of work: A-1 My Rack, A-2 Add equipment, A-3 Network settings, A-4 Connect equipment, A-5 Check firmware, then A-6 Prepare ESXi and A-7 Install Windows as hatched, locked tabs that state their reason. Each tab carries its status (✓ done, meaning saved in the plan; wash for the current step; hatch for locked), and every Continue goes to the next tab on the right and only moves, never saves. Each sheet has one primary action. Each sheet ends in a key strip and a title block: kit, sheet, revision, drawn by, checked by, date. The Checked-by box has a "Sign this sheet" control; signing records review only and proves nothing about hardware. Unsaved edits sit inside a revision cloud with a delta tag. The rack is drawn front-on, with U ticks and the overall dimension on the left, a front marker above it, and a leader line from the drop preview to its callout. Things that have answered a check are drawn in solid ink; plan-only equipment is drawn in dashed graphite. Examples of other states (a refused first contact, the erase dialog) sit in a separate band marked EXAMPLE, below the live sheet.

Evidence. Green means only today's proof for this exact server, always with its time: reached 25-09 08:35, iLO 5 v1.37, System ROM U32 v1.46. There is one rotated rubber stamp per group ("REACHED 25-09 08:35") and an upright green tick on each row. The model, bay count and drive count are not in that proof, so they show as saved plan or blank until Refresh drive inventory reads them. The erase of 24-09 is a recorded result, not a live check: it is drawn as an ink, dashed "recorded" mark that always carries its date and the words "recorded result, not a live check", and never in green. Recorded events (requested, power-on requested) are ink marks. Saved-plan values are pencil: graphite, italic, a slash mark, and stored inputs stay graphite until edited. Suggested values sit in a dashed box labelled "suggested". Blank values are upright, with a dash and no slash. Reasons are set in italic serif. Switches, sign-off boxes and decoration never use green.

Plan versus record. The saved plan marks 8 of 10 bays occupied; the recorded erase covered 6 reviewed disks at controller locations 1I:1:1 … 2I:1:8. They are shown side by side as two different records, never as two counts of today's drives, and controller locations are never mapped to front bay numbers. There is no current drive count until Refresh drive inventory runs.

Corrected 2026-09-25 after evidence review: erase wording, plan vs recorded disk count, commands, proof colour, alerts.

Type. Fraunces for titles, equipment names, glosses and explanations; IBM Plex Mono for values, labels, buttons and stamps. Scale: 11 / 13 / 15 / 17 / 24, with 30 for the kit title. Fallbacks: Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia, and Consolas / Menlo.
