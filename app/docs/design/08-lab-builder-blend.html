Something that speaks iLO answered at this address, so the network path works. The credentials were not accepted. Whether it is the server you meant is not known until a sign-in succeeds.

Design note · Lab Builder Studio

The idea

One light product page per step. The white-metal rack is the hero, drawn as a product shot with the project's own equipment art. A calm guide sits in the same place at the top of every screen: one plain sentence, one orange button, one line that says what will not change. Mission Control's frame stays around it: build steps with device lights on the left, and on the right an evidence drawer showing exactly what the last read-only check returned.

Orange means only “do this next”. Its complement, a deep ocean blue, carries structure, selection and saved-plan marks. Sand marks recorded results, and green is kept for today's proof on the exact device, always with its time.

Type

Fraunces is the guide's voice: a warm serif for the one sentence. Geist is used for every label, value and control, with tabular figures. Geist Mono appears only where exact characters matter: controller locations, disk models, the command preview and small eyebrows.

Palette · light (the hero)

Where each part came from

  • Mission Control: the build-steps rail with device lights, the evidence drawer (fold it with E), the command palette (Ctrl K), keyboard hints on primary actions, the kit menu and the reports links in the top bar, the three-number drives summary.
  • Companion: one sentence and one button per state, the reassurance line, “How we got here” written as sentences, each label explained the first time it appears, plain definitions beside jargon (iLO, RAID, U).
  • The original app: the equipment art, the white-metal rack, the orange, the six-step setup, drag-and-drop rack building with a 4 to 60 U height control.
