Lab Picture Book

An illustrated instruction manual for someone who has never opened a server. Numbered steps, a rack you tap, one colour for each kind of equipment, and only short labels and verbs. Green means one thing: the hardware said so, and the time is printed on it. The app frame is fluid: on a tablet or phone its columns stack into one, and only the rack drawing or a wide table scrolls inside its own box.

Designer's note

Audience & metaphor

A complete non-IT person who learns by looking. The app is an illustrated instruction manual: every screen is a numbered step, the rack is a physical object with numbered slots you tap, each kind of equipment has one colour and one picture, and copy is short labels and verbs. Plain words lead; the technical term follows as a small grey caption. Green means only "the hardware said so", always with its time. A step you finished by saving gets an ink tick, never green. Records from earlier days are double-ruled and dated, with a calendar mark instead of a tick. Blocked things stay on screen, dashed, with the reason. The boxed step says "You are here"; the orange dot and "Next to do" mark the step the build is waiting on.

Corrected 2026-09-25 after evidence review: erase wording, plan vs recorded disk count, commands, proof colour, alerts.

Equipment colours belong to equipment only. Every action button is ink. Warnings are apricot with an amber band and "!". Dark page: outlines flip to cream, offset shadows become slate #4d5175 so raised buttons still stand off the card, and the drop-preview hatch uses the soft line colour.

Type

Fredoka (500–700) for headings, numbers, labels and buttons: rounded and sturdy, like stencilled instruction text. Nunito (500–800) for the few sentences. Addresses, versions and part numbers are set in a monospace (Cascadia Mono, Consolas, ui-monospace) so 1, I and l stay apart. Five sizes only: 13 / 15 / 18 / 22 / 30px, nothing below 13.

Layout: 3–4px outlines, hard offset shadows, 14–18px radii, 48px touch targets. The frame is fluid. Below 1100px every workflow column stacks into one and the step strip wraps to three across; below 800px, two across. Only the rack drawing and the table below scroll inside their own box; the page never scrolls sideways.
